COMMISSION ORDER TOS Please read before you order
#RulesDetails
1 Payment Local clients: Purchases under 50k require full payment. Purchases above 50k require a 50% deposit. International clients: A 50% deposit or 100% full payment is required.
2 Revisions Free revisions follow the package you choose (e.g., lighting adjust, minor pose tweaks). Extra revisions or changes after final render/animation export will incur an additional fee.
3 Asset & Models Clients must provide valid Minecraft skins (128x128 / 512x512 / 64x64 PNG) or custom models (.obj/.bbmodel). If complex custom rigging, custom blocks, or special maps are required, additional charges or processing time may apply.
4 Deadlines Delivery time starts once the down payment and all required assets (skins/maps) are received or Orders are placed into a queue and processed sequentially. Heavy animations, complex lighting (ray-tracing), 4K renders, or a long queue list may extend delivery time. Check the "Queue Status" link for your current position.
5 Changes & Storyboard Approval Major changes to camera angles, animation keyframes, or scenery after the draft pose/storyboard preview is approved will be counted and charged as a new commission.
6 Refunds Refunds are only possible before work starts. Once work is in progress, the down payment (DP) is strictly non-refundable. If you paid in full upfront, a 50% partial refund of the total payment will be issued. A 100% full refund will be provided by the worker if they are unable to complete or take on your commission request.
7 Usage & Credit All services already include commercial use rights. However, you are kindly requested to always give proper credit to the animator or render artist whenever you post or use the work.
8 Content I do not accept NSFW, hateful, political, or illegal content. I reserve the right to decline any commission request.
9 Queue Orders are processed in sequential queue order. You can check the "View Queue Status" link to see the current list and your placement..
